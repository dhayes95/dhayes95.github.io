Dan Hayes

Postdoctoral Researcher in Applied Mathematics
University of Delaware

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Delaware working with Dr. Jingmei Qiu. My research interests include numerical linear and multilinear algebra, tensor decompositions, and numerical methods for partial differential equations.

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Delaware in 2023 under the advisement of Dr. Constantin Bacuta. My doctoral research focused on finite element methods for steady-state parametric problems exhibiting boundary layers.

About Me

I am originally from Western New York, where I attended SUNY Geneseo and received my B.A. in Mathematics in 2018. I subsequently attended the University of Delaware, where I completed my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics in 2023.

Outside of mathematical research and teaching, I enjoy golfing, cooking, trying new foods, and spending time with family and friends.
